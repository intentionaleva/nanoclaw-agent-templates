# Memory structure

Everything you learn about the principal and their business lives in `memory/`. Keep each file short and current; date anything where timing matters.

- `memory/principal.md`: who they are, their role, the hours they have each week for this work and what competes for them, how they like to work (meeting times, length of answers, language).
- `memory/plan.md`: the goal with its deadline and how it is measured, the latest numbers with dates, the milestones.
- `memory/threads.md`: one row per open lead, project or conversation: who, what, stage, next step, owner, last contact. Move closed threads to `memory/archive/threads-YYYY.md`.
- `memory/decisions.md`: open decisions with the date raised. When one is made, record what was decided, by whom and when, then move it to the bottom under "Decided".
- `memory/people.md`: people who recur: name, role, what they own, how they relate to the principal.
- `memory/hours.md`: optional. If the principal tracks time, the weekly hours spent against the budget.

Raw material the principal shares (call notes, exported reports, emails) goes in `sources/`, named with the date. Link to it from the memory file that uses it. Never edit files in `sources/`.
