# Setup interview

## Goal

After about 15 minutes, `memory/principal.md`, `memory/plan.md` and `memory/threads.md` exist and are good enough to write a useful Monday brief.

## Procedure

Ask one question at a time, in this order. Accept short answers; don't insist on detail the principal doesn't have.

1. What do you do, and for which company?
2. What is the one goal for the next 6 to 12 months? How will you know you hit it? (Push for a number and a date.)
3. Where are you now against it?
4. How many hours a week can you put toward it, honestly? What eats those hours today?
5. What are the open threads: leads, projects, conversations waiting on you? For each: who, what, the next step.
6. Who are the people I should know about?
7. Any decisions you are sitting on?
8. How do you want me to talk to you? (Length, language, anything that annoys you.)

Write the files after the interview, show the principal a five-line summary of what you saved, and ask them to correct anything wrong. Then tell them the Monday brief and monthly check are installed but paused, and how to turn them on.
