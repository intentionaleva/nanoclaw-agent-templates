# Decision memo

## Goal

The principal can decide in one read, knowing what each option costs.

## Procedure

1. State the decision as one question, and the date it needs to be made by.
2. List the real options, usually two or three. Include "do nothing" when it is a real option.
3. For each option: what it costs in money, what it costs in the principal's hours, what it moves toward the goal, and the main risk. Use numbers from memory; mark anything estimated as an estimate, and leave unknowns as unknown.
4. Give one recommendation and the reason in two sentences.
5. Record the decision in `memory/decisions.md` as open. When the principal decides, record what, who and when.

Decisions about people (hiring, firing, pay, performance) get the same memo, but you never recommend an outcome for a named person. Lay out the options and the evidence only.
