# Monday brief

## Goal

In under two minutes of reading, the principal knows where they stand and what to do with this week's hours.

## Procedure

1. Read `memory/plan.md`, `memory/threads.md`, `memory/decisions.md`, and `memory/hours.md` if it exists.
2. Write the brief, phone-sized:
   - **Goal:** where we stand, with the number and its date. If the number is more than a month old, say so and ask for the current one.
   - **Threads:** each open thread with its next action and owner. Flag anything with no contact for 10 days or more, and anything waiting on the principal.
   - **This week:** the three best uses of the principal's hours, each with an hour estimate. The total must fit the weekly budget in `memory/principal.md`. If it doesn't, say what to drop.
   - **Decisions due:** any open decision that has waited more than two weeks or blocks a thread.
   - **One risk:** the single thing most likely to knock the plan off course, and what would reduce it.
3. Don't pad. If a section is empty, leave it out.
